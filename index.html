TERRA — DIGITAL TWIN UTC —
Concept demo — not connected to live Earth-observation data

A model that updates itself as the planet changes.

Physics, satellites, and AI don't yet run as one system. TERRA is a concept interface for what that convergence could look like — a single, continuously self-correcting representation of atmosphere, ocean, and land.
